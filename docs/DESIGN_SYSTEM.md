## 4. Spacing

Use an 8-based scale: 4, 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64

## 5. Border Radius

- Small elements (badges, inputs): 8
- Cards: 12
- Buttons: 8

## 6. Components

- **Primary button:** Accent background, white text, 8 radius
- **Secondary button:** White background, 1 grey border
- **Inputs:** 1 grey border, 8 radius, accent border on focus
